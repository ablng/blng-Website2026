We design projects where the space is the business — the clearest expression of what an organisation is selling, arguing for, or building towards. Spatial identity gives an ambitious idea a coherent physical presence, so that the space itself carries the value of the business.

For commercial and workplace clients, the space is the largest single representation of the brand. For cultural clients, the space is the argument. For residential developers, identity is what attracts a premium-paying niche rather than a general market.

Our work here runs from the largest scale to the smallest: facade and massing, spatial sequence and circulation, material specification, and bespoke elements such as furniture, fittings, and ironmongery. We develop these against one brief, so the intelligence of the overall idea carries down to the detail.

Delivered within our architecture and interiors commissions, or as a standalone consultancy at specific project stages.
