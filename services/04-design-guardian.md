# Design Guardian (Client Design Advisory)

We provide independent client-side design leadership and strategic quality oversight within contractor-led, Design & Build (D&B), or multi-disciplinary delivery teams. Operating within the RIBA Client Design Adviser framework, we safeguard architectural integrity and Employer’s Requirements throughout technical design, procurement, value engineering, and construction.

Acting on the client's behalf, we provide objective design scrutiny at key project gateways to ensure the completed scheme achieves the spatial quality, material standards, and performance established at inception.

### What this covers

- **Client Design Representation:** Independent client-side design governance and advisory across RIBA Stages 0–6.
- **Preservation of Design Intent:** Defending Employer's Requirements and architectural standards through procurement and value engineering.
- **Technical Submittals Review:** Evaluating contractor design portions (CDP), technical submittals, and specification compliance.
- **Material Approvals & Mock-ups:** Benchmark reviews, sample sign-offs, and quality mock-up inspections.
- **Construction Quality Oversight:** On-site monitoring and design compliance reviews during the construction phase.
- **Strategic Design Counsel:** Impartial design advisory and brief defense for founders, boards, and family offices.
