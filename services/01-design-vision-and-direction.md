# Design Vision & Direction (Early Feasibility & Spatial Concept)

We translate early-stage ambition into formed architectural and spatial concepts. Working at project inception, site acquisition, or portfolio repositioning, we interrogate the brief, assess site capacity, and produce visual design proposals that establish spatial, material, and geometric direction.

### What this covers

- **Land, Site & Property Feasibility:** Early assessment of site capacity, constraints, and development potential.
- **Massing & Yield Studies:** Computational massing and spatial optimization to test project parameters.
- **Pre-Application Planning Review:** Initial planning context, urban analysis, and statutory alignment.
- **Spatial & Material Direction:** Establishing the architectural character, spatial sequence, and material palette.
- **Visual Concept Proposals:** Key spaces modeled and visualized to define spatial intent for stakeholders and investors.
- **Strategic Roadmap:** Clear guidance for subsequent design stages, cost parameters, and team assembly.
