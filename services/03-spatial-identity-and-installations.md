# Spatial Identity & Installations (Brand, Culture & Bespoke Objects)

We design spatial environments where physical identity defines the experience of an organization, brand, or institution. From commercial workplaces and hospitality to cultural pavilions and installations, we translate complex narratives into coherent material and spatial form.

Our work spans from architectural scale to bespoke detail, ensuring consistent design intent across space, material, and touchpoints.

### What this covers

- **Spatial Identity:** Translating brand ethos and narrative into physical volume, sequence, and material identity.
- **Workplace & Commercial Environments:** Environments designed around brand expression and occupational experience.
- **Installations & Pavilions:** Temporary and permanent architectural interventions, exhibition design, and cultural pavilions.
- **Bespoke Objects & Furniture:** Architectural hardware, custom fittings, limited-edition furniture, and maker collaborations.
- **Creative Direction:** Directing physical and visual touchpoints to maintain overarching design coherence.
