# Architecture & Interiors (Full Project Delivery, RIBA Stages 0–6)

We lead architectural and interior commissions from inception to completion across new build, retrofit, and adaptive reuse. Our directors are registered architects in the UK and Germany, delivering projects across both jurisdictions and internationally.

We operate across RIBA Stages 0–6 as lead architect or as design architect within multi-disciplinary teams, combining formal precision with sustainable construction and contextual sensitivity.

### What this covers

- **Strategic Briefing & Concept:** Project brief development, site analysis, spatial massing, architectural narrative, and initial visual studies.
- **Spatial Coordination & Planning:** Coordinated architectural and interior layouts, heritage and contextual integration, and statutory planning submissions.
- **Technical Design & Specification:** Comprehensive construction drawings, bespoke interior packages, material schedules, structural coordination, and tender documentation.
- **Construction Administration & Handover:** On-site quality monitoring, contractor technical reviews, mock-up sign-offs, contract administration, and final project handover.
