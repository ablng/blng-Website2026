Leo Bieling is a Principal at blng studio, co-founding the London practice and carrying forward an architectural lineage begun by his grandfather Josef in 1955. He brings inherited craft and computational precision to every project, from structural logic to surface detail.

Leo came to architecture through lived experience — years spent on construction sites and project visits with his father Thomas, where design, craft, and the ambition to build things that matter were simply part of daily life. Architecture was never abstract — it was a fundamental mindset for progress and innovation.

Before co-founding blng studio, Leo worked at Zaha Hadid Architects, Coop Himmelb(l)au, and Plus Architecture, contributing to international cultural, commercial, and competition projects and deepening his expertise in computational design and fabrication.

At blng, Leo is focused on establishing the studio as a design-led practice on both the local and international stage. An unwavering belief that no project is too small for design ambition.

For Leo, academia is the starting point for design research and ideas — a space where design thinking is tested, challenged, and advanced. He has been associated with the Architectural Association, UCL Bartlett, ETH Zurich, and Tsinghua University Beijing.

Outside the studio, Leo sails — a discipline that sharpens a hands-on understanding of structure, force, and material in the world as it actually is.

He holds an MArch from the Architectural Association, London, and an MSc with Distinction from TU Vienna. He is a registered architect in the UK (ARB/RIBA) and Germany (BDA).
